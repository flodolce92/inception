Welcome to 42 Roma

In the heart of Italy's eternal city, a revolution in tech education is taking place. 42 Roma represents a bold departure from traditional learning models, offering a completely free, peer-to-peer coding education that's transforming how future developers are trained.

A New Approach to Learning

42 Roma operates without teachers, without classes, and without tuition fees. Instead, students learn through project-based challenges and peer collaboration. This innovative model, born in Paris and now thriving in Rome, creates an environment where problem-solving and critical thinking are developed organically through hands-on experience.

The campus is open 24/7, allowing students to work at their own pace and on their own schedule. This flexibility attracts a diverse community of learners, from recent high school graduates to career changers seeking to break into the tech industry.

The Piscine Experience

Every journey at 42 Roma begins with "La Piscine" (The Pool) – an intensive 4-week selection process that serves as both an introduction and a trial by fire. Candidates dive deep into C programming, shell scripting, and fundamental computer science concepts. It's demanding, it's exhausting, but it's also where lifelong friendships are forged and true passion for coding is discovered.

The Piscine isn't just about testing technical skills; it evaluates resilience, collaboration ability, and the capacity to learn autonomously. Those who emerge from this crucible are ready to tackle the comprehensive curriculum that follows.

Peer-to-Peer Learning

At the core of 42's philosophy is peer evaluation. Students review each other's code, discuss solutions, and learn from diverse approaches to the same problem. This system mirrors real-world software development environments where code review and collaboration are essential skills.

"The best way to learn is to teach. At 42, every student is both a learner and a teacher."

— 42 Network Philosophy

The Campus Culture

Located in Rome's vibrant tech district, the 42 Roma campus buzzes with energy and creativity. Students don't just code – they organize hackathons, host tech talks, contribute to open-source projects, and build a community that extends far beyond the classroom walls.

The diversity of the student body enriches the learning experience. With no prerequisites beyond basic literacy and numeracy, 42 Roma welcomes individuals from all backgrounds, creating a melting pot of perspectives and experiences that drives innovation.

Career Opportunities

Graduates of 42 Roma are highly sought after by tech companies. The program's emphasis on practical skills, autonomous learning, and collaborative problem-solving produces developers who can adapt quickly to new technologies and work effectively in team environments.

From startups to multinational corporations, employers recognize the value of 42's unique training model. Students don't just learn to code – they learn to think like engineers, to persevere through challenges, and to continuously evolve their skills in an ever-changing technological landscape.

42 Roma isn't just a school – it's a launchpad for the next generation of Italian tech talent, proving that with the right environment and methodology, anyone with passion and determination can become a world-class developer.
